For induction heating systems that use susceptors to heat electrically non-conductive or poorly coupling charges (e.g., ceramics such as yttria-stabilized zirconia), review the literature on graphite and refractory-metal (e.g., tantalum) susceptor design and use: RF coupling efficiency, susceptor geometry and placement, heat transfer to the charge by conduction/radiation, achievable temperatures and operating limits at 1700-2500 C, and susceptor vapor deposition / contamination of the charge.
