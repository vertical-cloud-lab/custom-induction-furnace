## Methods and limitations

I parsed all **11,869** records in `rsi_all_articles_compact.tsv`, rather than starting from the 351 keyword-selected records. An independent title sweep used seven concept families: induction/RF heating; furnaces and heating stages; pyrometry/emissivity; levitation, floating-zone, laser heating, solidification, and sintering; vacuum/controlled atmosphere; EBSD/grain growth; and open-source control/data acquisition. This produced **737 title hits** for manual triage.

For each candidate I then checked:

1. whether the paper actually describes an instrument that was designed, built, calibrated, or operated, rather than merely mentioning temperature;
2. first/last/sole-author position as a rough indicator of leadership;
3. repeated RSI activity and activity in **2021 or later**;
4. topical complementarity across the five manuscript pillars;
5. full-name and affiliation data in `rsi_relevant_articles.jsonl`;
6. institutional pages or recent RSI affiliations for likely current institutions;
7. name-level coauthorship, manuscript citations, BYU affiliation, and links to the two flagged Baird groups.

This is a qualitative reviewer-selection problem, so inferential statistics, p-values, and model-based confidence intervals are not applicable. The ranking is evidence-weighted judgment, not a validated prediction of review quality.

**Known limitations:** affiliation fields are incomplete; author disambiguation in the full TSV is initials-based; first/last position is an imperfect proxy for seniority; 2026 records may include early-online material; public searches cannot reveal confidential or informal conflicts; and current appointment details remain uncertain for G. Lohöfer, Andreas Bauer, Jérôme Mendonça, and Sébastien Weber. The DOI claims above were all checked against the supplied corpus.

### Discretionary analytical decisions

- Defined seven independent title-search themes rather than reusing the supplied keyword score.
- Prioritized demonstrated instrument construction, calibration, or operation over incidental topical matches.
- Treated first, last, or sole authorship plus repeated RSI publication as evidence of reviewer seniority, while allowing exceptionally close first-author builders such as Mendonça and Bauer.
- Favored 2021+ activity but retained older exact matches when the apparatus was unusually close to the manuscript, especially Bauer’s UHV induction furnace.
- Ranked for complementary five-pillar coverage rather than selecting the 13 highest individual relevance scores.
- Kept only one primary candidate from closely overlapping groups where possible; Kargl was preferred over Dreißigacker, Gallais over Pontillon, and Bauer over Pfleiderer.
- Retained Zaefferer despite only one corpus paper because EBSD expertise is otherwise nearly absent from RSI’s 2015–2026 author pool.
- Treated unresolved same-surname/initial cases conservatively and did not recommend the ambiguous aerodynamic-levitation “R. Weber.”
