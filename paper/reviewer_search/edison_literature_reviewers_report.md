### Publication-verification notes

All DOIs in the table were matched to bibliographic records that explicitly name the proposed reviewer; none was assigned merely from subject similarity. One correction to the compact table is important: Shukla’s peer-reviewed *Review of Scientific Instruments* article is **“Design, development, and performance of a versatile graphene epitaxy system for the growth of epitaxial graphene on SiC” (2024), DOI 10.1063/5.0194852**. The table displays the related arXiv DOI because that was the full-text version retrieved, but the AIP DOI should be used in the reviewer nomination. The retrieved paper confirms Shukla’s CSIR-NPL/AcSIR affiliations and the system’s RF-induction, vacuum/argon, automation, and 2000 °C capabilities. (mondal2024designfabricationand pages 1-4)

The Wang–Watkins–Trofimov study directly compares two-color and one-color pyrometers, an IR camera, and a Pyrolaser under realistic line-of-sight interference. It discusses emissivity, transmissivity corrections, blackbody calibration, furnaces, induction processing, and measurement uncertainty, making any one of these authors technically well suited to assess the manuscript’s ratio-pyrometer/PID implementation. (trofimov2023infraredthermometryin pages 1-6) Araújo’s two DOI-verified papers are particularly close to RSI/MST’s remit: multi-wavelength surface-temperature estimation and dual-band-pyrometry uncertainty.

The high-temperature emissivity review by Giulietti, Revel, Chiariotti and colleagues addresses methods, uncertainty, furnaces, and measurements up to 2500 °C. It therefore supports Revel, Chiariotti, or Giulietti as independent metrology reviewers, although only one should normally be invited from that paper’s author group. (giulietti2025spectralemissivitymeasurement pages 1-2)

For microstructure validation, the Ni-200 study explicitly combines controlled annealing with repeated EBSD measurements and quantifies grain-size distributions and coincident-site-lattice boundaries. This is the closest retrieved match to the nickel grain-growth portion. (underwood2016anexaminationof pages 1-2) Rohrer is a broader EBSD/grain-boundary specialist whose recent work develops stereological analysis of EBSD orientation maps and discusses applications to zirconia as well as other polycrystals. (folwarczny2026accurategrainboundary pages 1-3)

For the 2500 °C YSZ extension, Luo’s work includes carbon-heater processing near 3000 K and rapid liquid-phase-assisted sintering, while his reactive flash-SPS paper combines UHTC synthesis, rapid heating, EBSD, and grain-level characterization. (xie2022rapidliquidphase–assisted pages 1-2, gild2019reactiveflashspark pages 1-2) Marinel and Biesuz provide complementary expertise in rapid sintering, electromagnetic/dielectric heating, susceptors, atmosphere-controlled furnaces, and grain-growth control. (karacasulu2025sinteringunderhigh pages 1-2) Sciti’s work directly concerns manufacturing and testing ceramic composites intended for service above 2000 °C. (zoli2021ultrahightemperatureceramic pages 1-4)

Pearce and Hubbard are valuable specifically for the manuscript’s retrofit and reproducible-hardware aspects: their open-source vacuum oven uses off-the-shelf and additively manufactured components and reports performance and cost. Its temperature range is much lower than the submitted instrument’s, so either should be paired with an induction or UHT reviewer. (hubbard2021opensourcevacuum pages 1-2)

## Practical invitation strategy

A balanced three-reviewer panel would be: **Shukla** for the integrated RF/vacuum/argon apparatus; **Wang or Araújo** for pyrometry and uncertainty; and **Madison, Luo, or Rohrer** depending on whether the editor wants greater emphasis on Ni grain-growth validation, UHT zirconia processing, or EBSD methodology. Do not simultaneously invite close substitutes from the same collaboration cluster: Wang/Watkins/Trofimov, Revel/Chiariotti/Giulietti, Luo/Vecchio, or Marinel/Biesuz. Authors merely cited by the manuscript are not conflicted on that basis; the editorial record should mark Araújo or any other cited candidate as **cited in manuscript** if confirmed from the submitted reference list.
