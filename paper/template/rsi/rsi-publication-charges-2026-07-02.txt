Publication Charges

Review of Scientific Instruments
.
Open Access Charges
By electing to participate in
Author Select
®
and paying a 3800 USD fee to support publication and archiving costs, authors may ask the journal to provide free online access to a published article, in perpetuity, to any online user. Visit the
AIP Publishing Open Access Policy
for more information about our policies on open access. When you submit a new or revised manuscript, let us know if you would like to participate in Author Select
®
.
Color Figures
Illustrations will appear in color in the online journal free of charge to authors. To take advantage of this free service, authors must do the following:
Send usable color graphic files (.eps, .ps, .tif, or .pdf only).
Send graphic files during the production process.
Send only one version of each graphic file.
Ensure that color graphics will reproduce well when printed in black & white.
All figures will appear in black and white in the printed journal.
There is a surcharge for ordering full-color author reprints.
